# Design context · Destination Rolodex (Uno Platform)

Source: the creator's briefs in `../briefs` (01-product, 02-design, 05-motion, revision 2 Living Postcard brief).
Written 2026-10-08 so design skills can read the context without re-deriving it from code.

## Target audience
- Developers evaluating Uno Platform: they open the demo page, scroll the card, switch Morph Blend / Liquid Warp,
  scrub a frame, and read the component API. Secondary: the Uno Platform content team recording the card for
  articles and social clips (desktop window, 1440 wide, and a 390 wide phone frame).

## Use cases
- Browse six destinations by wheel, drag, keys or the arrows; feel the photo effect follow the gesture.
- Compare the two approved effects on the same pair at the same duration and intensity; replay and scrub.
- Drop `DestinationRolodexCard` into a XAML page and bind a destination list.

## Brand personality and tone
- "Curiosity, warmth, and quiet wonder." Light, quiet, compact. The photograph is the emphasis; chrome stays secondary.
- Editorial postcard: serif place caption against a tracked sans country list; warm cream rim; low-saturation
  material colors mixed from each destination's authored pigment. Nothing glowing, glassy or dark.
- Page (as of 2026-10-08, user decision): slate grey-white `#E6E8EC` behind a cream-rimmed card.
- Banned by the creator: travel logos, flag icons, orbit lines, extra buttons, marketing copy, dark glass concepts.

## Quality bar
- Flagship sample: this card is recorded and published. Key moments: the gesture-driven photo effect with photo-in-type,
  and the arrival settle. Everything else matches platform defaults cleanly.

## Type and tokens
- Inter static instances (card and page); Source Serif 4 Regular for the place caption (stands in for Georgia).
- Tokens live in `DestinationRolodex/Styles/DestinationRolodexResources.xaml` (Rolodex* and Postcard*).
