Behind the destinations

Each country has its own background photo. The images load online; the card, controls, and animation run without a framework.

    Look around adds a constrained pan, zoom, and perspective effect to a flat photograph. It is not a 360° panorama or a reconstructed 3D scene. A small crop of your reference image is included as the initial Philippines loading preview. The morph uses procedural texture displacement, not landmark tracking or true 3D geometry. Photo shaders need WebGL and cross-origin image permission; otherwise the original crossfade remains available.
